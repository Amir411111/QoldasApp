# 🕒 QoldasApp — Sheduling App for your soul

---

## 📱 Overview

**QoldasApp** is an AI-powered time management application that helps users efficiently plan their day, prioritize tasks, and reduce procrastination.  
Designed primarily for students, it uses artificial intelligence to generate an **optimized daily schedule** based on a user’s task list, preferences, and priorities.

The app aims to enhance productivity, improve mood, and decrease stress by automating complex decision-making processes related to daily planning.

---

## 🧠 Key Features

* 🤖 **AI Scheduling Assistant:** Automatically generates personalized daily plans based on your tasks.  
* 🗓️ **Task Prioritization:** Uses AI logic to structure tasks by importance and urgency.  
* 🔔 **Reminders & Notifications:** Keeps users on track with upcoming deadlines and events.  
* 📊 **Productivity Tracking:** Monitors user performance, stress, and mood over time.  
* 🌙 **User-Friendly Interface:** Simple, clean, and intuitive design for students and professionals.  

---

## 🧩 Motivation

Time management is a vital skill that directly affects productivity, academic performance, and mental well-being.  
However, surveys show that **over 60% of students** use time management apps ineffectively, mainly due to poor task prioritization.  
**QoldasApp** addresses this by offering **AI-driven scheduling** that adapts to user behavior and improves planning efficiency.

---

## 📊 Research & Findings

A study conducted among **NIS Semey** students (grades 7–12) revealed the following:

* 61.3% of students use time management apps but find them ineffective.  
* 71.3% identified incorrect task prioritization as the main challenge.  
* 81.3% are open to AI assistance for scheduling.  

After a 30-day experiment using QoldasApp:

* ⏫ Productivity doubled (from 2.5 → 4.9/5)  
* 😌 Stress decreased by 55%  
* 😊 Mood improved by 60%  

These results confirmed that **QoldasApp** significantly enhances efficiency and emotional well-being.
